Who is Nandan Nilekani?

Nandan Nilekani is an Indian entrepreneur, technologist, and visionary leader who co-founded Infosys, one of India's largest and most respected IT services companies. He currently serves as the non-executive Chairman of Infosys Technologies Limited. Beyond his corporate achievements, Nilekani is widely recognized as the architect of Aadhaar, India's revolutionary biometric identity system that has provided unique identification to over 1.4 billion people.

He served as the Founding Chairman of the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) from 2009 to 2014 in the rank of a Cabinet Minister. Nilekani is also an acclaimed author, philanthropist, and public policy advocate who has been listed among TIME magazine's 100 most influential people in the world twice (2006 and 2009).

Known For: Co-founding Infosys, creating India's Aadhaar identity system, championing digital public infrastructure, and authoring influential books on India's future.

Early Life & Education

Nandan Nilekani was born on 2 June 1955 in Bangalore, Karnataka, India. He belongs to a Konkani Brahmin family originally from Sirsi in Karnataka. His father, Mohan Rao Nilekani, worked as a general manager of Mysore and Minerva Mills and was known for his Fabian Socialist ideals, which greatly influenced young Nandan's worldview and social thinking in his formative years.

Growing up in Bangalore, Nilekani received his early education at Bishop Cotton Boys' School, one of the oldest and most prestigious schools in the city. The school's rigorous academic environment nurtured his intellectual curiosity and discipline from an early age.

For his higher education, Nilekani went on to pursue a Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree in Electrical Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bombay, one of India's premier engineering institutions. He graduated in 1978, a year that would prove pivotal in shaping his future career and India's IT industry.

Career

Early Career at Patni Computer Systems (1978-1981)

After graduating from IIT Bombay in 1978, Nandan Nilekani joined Patni Computer Systems in Mumbai. It was here that he was interviewed by and met N.R. Narayana Murthy, a meeting that would eventually change the course of India's technology landscape. The two developed a strong professional bond and shared a vision for what Indian IT could become on the global stage.

Co-founding Infosys (1981)

In 1981, Nilekani, along with N.R. Narayana Murthy and five other colleagues, left Patni Computer Systems to start their own company. With an initial capital of just US$250 (borrowed from Murthy's wife), they founded Infosys in Pune, India. The company was built on the principles of integrity, transparency, and delivering world-class IT services.

What began as a small startup with seven co-founders grew into one of the largest and most respected IT companies in the world. Infosys pioneered the Global Delivery Model (GDM) and became a symbol of India's software export success story.

CEO of Infosys (2002-2007)

Nandan Nilekani served as the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Infosys from March 2002 to April 2007. Under his leadership, the company witnessed massive growth, strengthened its global presence, and became India's second-largest IT services company. He played a crucial role in establishing Infosys as a global brand and a model for corporate governance in India.

During his tenure as CEO, Nilekani was known for his strategic vision, client-centric approach, and ability to foresee major technology trends. His leadership helped Infosys earn the trust of Fortune 500 clients across the world.

Return as Chairman (2017-Present)

In August 2017, following a period of leadership turmoil at Infosys and the exit of CEO Vishal Sikka, Nilekani was appointed as the non-executive Chairman of the Board. His return brought stability and renewed confidence among investors, employees, and clients. He continues to serve in this capacity, guiding the company's strategic direction in the age of AI and digital transformation.

Aadhaar & Public Service

In 2009, Nandan Nilekani took on one of the most ambitious public service projects in history. He was appointed as the Founding Chairman of the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) in the rank of a Cabinet Minister by the Government of India. His mission was to create a unique identity system for every resident of India.

Under his leadership, the Aadhaar project was conceptualized and executed, eventually enrolling over 1.4 billion people with a unique 12-digit identity number linked to their biometric data. Aadhaar has since become the foundation of India's digital public infrastructure, enabling direct benefit transfers, financial inclusion, and efficient delivery of government services to millions of citizens.

Nilekani served as UIDAI Chairman until 2014. The Aadhaar system is widely considered one of the most transformative digital governance initiatives in the world, and Nilekani is globally recognized as its principal architect.

Political Foray (2014)

In 2014, Nilekani contested the Lok Sabha elections from the Bangalore South constituency on an Indian National Congress ticket. However, he was defeated by BJP candidate Ananth Kumar. Despite the electoral loss, his contribution to public policy and governance through technology remains widely acknowledged.

Personal Life & Family

Nandan Nilekani is married to Rohini Nilekani, a renowned author, journalist, and philanthropist. Rohini is the founder of Arghyam Foundation, a non-profit that focuses on water and sanitation issues in India. She also chairs the Akshara Foundation, which works towards improving elementary education. The couple is known for their deep commitment to philanthropy and social impact.

Together, they have two children:

  • Nihar Nilekani - Their son
  • Janhavi Nilekani - Their daughter, who is the founder of Aastrika Foundation, which works in the area of maternal and newborn health

The Nilekani family is based in Bangalore, Karnataka, and is known for their understated lifestyle despite immense wealth. They are among India's most generous philanthropic families.

Net Worth

As of 2025, Nandan Nilekani's estimated net worth is approximately $3.2 billion (USD), making him the 100th richest person in India according to Forbes. His wealth primarily stems from his shareholding in Infosys Technologies, one of India's most valuable companies listed on both the BSE/NSE (India) and NYSE (United States).

Books & Publications

Nandan Nilekani is also an accomplished author. His books reflect his deep thinking on India's future, technology, and governance:

  • Imagining India: The Idea of a Renewed Nation (2008) - His debut book exploring the challenges and opportunities facing India in the 21st century. It became a bestseller and was widely praised for its visionary outlook.
  • Rebooting India: Realizing a Billion Aspirations (2015) - Co-authored with Viral Shah, this book discusses how technology can be used to transform governance and public services in India.
  • The Art of Bitfulness: Keeping Calm in the Digital World (2022) - Co-authored with Tanuj Bhojwani, this book offers practical advice on maintaining balance and well-being in an increasingly digital world.
